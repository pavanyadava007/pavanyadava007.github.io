Featured project

offroad-bevfusion — camera + LiDAR + radar multitask BEV perception

Four replay segments: urban perception + VLA · hazard drive (Qwen2.5-VL LoRA VLA + deterministic fail-safe rule escalating APPROACH → WAIT → STOP as a pedestrian enters the corridor; VLA grounded on GT perception) · night/wet scene · GOOSE off-road 8-class BEV segmentation in rain.

An open-source, end-to-end off-highway perception stack built on a zero-cost GPU budget: six cameras (LSS), LiDAR pillars and 5-sweep radar pillars fused into a shared 200×200 BEV by a deformable-attention encoder, feeding 3D detection (CenterPoint), BEV segmentation and 3D occupancy heads under Kendall uncertainty-weighted multitask loss. Exported as static-shape ONNX and deployed as TensorRT engines behind a ROS 2 Humble C++ node — no custom plugins, CI-tested export parity. Includes a 14-stage DVC data pipeline with hard-example mining, adverse-weather and radar-dropout robustness studies, and a VLA task-grounding interface for wheel-loader action primitives with an ISO 21448 hazard mapping.
